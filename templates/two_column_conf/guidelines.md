# Two-Column AI/ML Conference Guidelines

- **Format**: Double-column US Letter (`10pt`), double-blind anonymized submission (`Anonymous Submission`).
- **Page Limit**: Maximum 8 pages for main text (including figures and tables, excluding references and appendices).
- **Layout Discipline**: Prefer single-column `\begin{figure}[t]` with `[width=\linewidth]`; use `\begin{figure*}[t]` only for wide architecture or multi-panel comparisons. Zero `Overfull \hbox` exceeding `15pt` into column gutters.
- **Tables**: Strictly `booktabs` (`\toprule`, `\midrule`, `\bottomrule`); resize wide tables cleanly or split logically rather than overflowing margins.
- **Citations & Anonymity**: Never reveal author identities, institutions, or grant numbers; cite prior work objectively in third person.
